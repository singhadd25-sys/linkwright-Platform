Dock pre-prepared PDBQT ligands against one pre-prepared receptor. The receptor and ligands must already have correct atom types, charges, protonation and hydrogens. This page does not prepare structures or identify the binding site.

Scientific cautions
  • Vina scores are approximate ranking estimates, not binding free energies.
  • Use a redocking benchmark and report pose RMSD where a reference pose exists.
  • Inspect protonation, cofactors, metals, waters and receptor flexibility manually.
  • Docking does not establish activity, selectivity or safety.
